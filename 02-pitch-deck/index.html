The amenity above everything.

Vantage designs, builds and installs rooftop sauna suites for apartment buildings, hotels and offices.

Vantage Sauna Co. · Confidential · Jacob [Last name], Founder & CEO

Open with the view: every large building has a roof it does nothing with, and every owner is looking for a wellness amenity. We put the two together. Vantage is a product company, not a spa operator: we build, install and service rooftop sauna suites, and the building owns the amenity.

The problem

Owners have run out of amenities to add.

Amenities decide leases

Gyms, lounges and coworking floors are table stakes. Owners need something residents, guests and tenants talk about.

The big options are gone

A rooftop pool costs millions and takes years. A spa floor needs plumbing, mechanical and space the building does not have.

The roof sits empty

A mechanical zone and nothing else, or a deck used three months a year. The best view in the building, unused.

Today a rooftop sauna means an architect, a structural engineer, a sauna builder, an electrician, a crane company, several permits and about a year. Nobody sells it as a product.

02

Three forces: amenities are how large buildings compete, the expensive amenities are out of reach, and the roof is the one space left. Then the punchline: the only way to get a rooftop sauna today is a custom project with half a dozen contractors.

The solution

A rooftop sauna suite as a product: one contract, one price, six to ten weeks.

All-electric. UL 875 listed commercial heater. No gas, no flue, no combustion on the roof.

Factory-built. Craned as a module or carried up the freight elevator as a flat-pack. One to six days on site.

Zero roof penetrations. Load-spreading steel base, ballast and parapet tie-back, reviewed by a structural engineer on every site.

Run by software. Residents and guests book in the Vantage Access app; the smart lock, session timer and emergency stop do the rest.

Serviced for life. Vantage Care: quarterly inspections, heater stones, bench refinishing, 24-hour support.

Business model

Hardware sale up front, recurring revenue for the life of the building.

1. Purchase

50% at contract, 40% at delivery, 10% at commissioning. Deposits fund the build, so working capital stays light.

2. Vantage Flex lease

From $1,650, $2,950 or $5,200 a month over 84 months, Care and Access included. A finance partner pays Vantage in full at install.

3. Hotel revenue share (5 pilots)

$0 upfront, 35% of session revenue, $1,500 a month minimum, five-year term. Hotels price sessions at $35–$60 a guest.

09

The honest answer on competition: premium prefab makers could try to move up into rooftops, and they are our most likely acquirers. Our defence is the rooftop engineering kit, the code package, the installed base on software and service, and references from the first forty buildings.

Why it is hard to copy

The product is the sauna. The moat is everything around it.

1. Rooftop engineering kit

Steel base frame, ballast trays and parapet tie-backs, pre-engineered for occupiable roofs rated 100 psf, carrying the load to beams and columns. Stamped per site in days, not months.

2. Code and permit package

ETL/UL 875 listing of the whole module (heater plus prefabricated room, as Edition 10 allows), NEC 422/424 electrical design, non-combustible envelope and permit templates per metro.

3. Two ways onto the roof

Freight-elevator flat-pack by default (no crane permits in dense cities) or a craned module, from the same panel system. Backyard sauna makers need a driveway.

4. The installed base

Every building runs on Vantage Access and Care. Usage data, references and portfolio relationships compound with each roof.

Certification, the structural kit and the code package are the first things this seed round pays for.
