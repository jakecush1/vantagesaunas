Vantage Sauna Co. · Seed round

09

We found no Canadian firm that sells a turnkey rooftop suite, and only one commercial rooftop sauna in the three cities, at a downtown Vancouver hotel, probably site-built. The nearest commercial product is a delivered-only cabin from about C$78,000; our Perch is about C$21,000 more installed, and that buys the structural and seismic check, permit drawings, the lift onto the roof, the electrical connection, commissioning and software. A strata council will compare us to a C$10,000 barrel; the answer is commercial duty and a Canadian-marked heater. For hotels the real alternative is the social-sauna boom down the street, so we position Vantage as the building's own amenity. The honest risk: a prefab maker could move up into rooftops. Our defence is the seismic kit, the certification work, the installed base and the first references.
