This is the base plan from our quarterly cash model, in Canadian dollars. Installs grow from 5 in 2027 to 45 in 2031, with Toronto taking over as the largest city from 2029. Hardware carries the revenue; Care and Access carry the valuation story and reach about C$1M a year by 2031. Gross margin starts near 19 percent on the pilot batch, because early units cost more and pilots get a 15 percent discount, then reaches 40 percent in 2029 and 42 percent by 2031. Cash never drops below C$0.84M, in the second quarter of 2029, which is about six months of costs. The downside case, the research's conservative 81 buildings, only breaks even by 2031; the seed still lasts through 2029, and we would delay the 2029 and 2030 hires.
