The first 12 months

From incorporation to the first paid installs.

Nov 2026 – Jan 2027

  • Incorporate in BC; ask for a PST ruling
  • Lawyer, SR&ED accountant, BC structural engineer
  • 30 meetings with owners and hotels
  • Head of engineering hired

Gate: 3 signed letters of intent by end of January

Feb – Apr 2027

Fund and open the workshop

  • Seed closes by end of March
  • CSBFP loan for tools and truck
  • Workshop in Langley or Abbotsford
  • Sales lead starts in April

Done when: C$2.0M in the bank, loan approved

May – Jul 2027

Build the demo Lodge

  • Canadian-marked heaters and tinted glass
  • Demo Lodge built and heat-tested
  • Permit drawing set ready
  • Operations manager starts in July

Gate: demo Lodge costs C$130,000 or less

Aug – Oct 2027

First paid installs

  • Permits in Vancouver and Victoria
  • Two suites installed in Q3
  • Vantage Care live
  • Three more contracts for Q4

Done when: 2 suites live, zero safety incidents

If a gate fails: fewer than 3 letters of intent means we change the offer before raising; a demo over C$130,000 means we redesign the panels before more orders; fewer than 3 of the 5 pilots willing to buy again by end of 2027 means we pause Toronto.
