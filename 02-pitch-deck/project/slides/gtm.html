Go-to-market

Start where one person signs. Vancouver and Victoria first, then Toronto.

Who we sell to, in order

  1. Rental towers and business or serviced apartments: one owner decides
  2. Upscale and lifestyle hotels: revenue-share pilots
  3. Condos and strata: slower, with a meeting kit for BC and Ontario votes
  4. Developers of new rental and hotel projects

How we reach them

  • Direct sales to owners and asset managers
  • Property and strata management firms
  • Architects and developers, briefed in 2027 for 2029–2031 openings
  • A demo Lodge in the Fraser Valley and a lighthouse rooftop in Vancouver

Launch plan

  • 2027: Vancouver and Victoria, 5 paid installs, 3 lighthouse references
  • 2028: Toronto sales from Q2; flat-packs ship by truck
  • Toronto is about 60% of the suitable pool
  • Sales cycle 2–4 months for rentals and hotels, 4–9 for condos

One owner often controls many buildings. The first install in a portfolio is a sale; the rest are a roll-out.

We lead with purpose-built rental towers and serviced apartments, because one owner decides and new towers are fighting for tenants. Hotels come second, through up to three revenue-share pilots. Condos are the biggest pool but need a vote: three quarters of the votes cast in BC, and owners of two thirds of all units in Ontario when the cost is over 10 percent of the budget, so we bring an information-session kit and sample resolutions. Vancouver and Victoria share one province, one strata law and one building code, so we launch there in 2027. Toronto is the volume market and opens in 2028; flat-pack kits travel by truck from the Fraser Valley. Nobody buys a sauna from a brochure, which is why the demo Lodge matters.
