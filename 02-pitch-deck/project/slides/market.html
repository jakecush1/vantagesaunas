The universe is sourced; the suitable filter is ours and we say so. Rental towers and hotels are a third of the suitable pool but most of the early sales, because one owner decides. Condos are the largest pool and the slowest sale, because they need an owner vote. Toronto holds about 60 percent of the suitable buildings, which is why we add it in 2028 once Vancouver and Victoria have references. The base plan of 122 buildings by 2031 is about 11 percent of the suitable market; the research's conservative case is 81.
