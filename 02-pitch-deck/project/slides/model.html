Business model

Hardware sale up front, recurring revenue for the life of the building.

1. Purchase

50% at contract, 40% at delivery, 10% at commissioning. Deposits pay for materials. Prices exclude GST/PST/HST.

2. Vantage Flex lease

From C$2,150, C$3,850 or C$6,800 a month over 84 months, Care and Access included. A Canadian finance partner pays Vantage in full at install.

3. Hotel revenue share (up to 3 pilots)

C$0 upfront, 35% of session revenue, C$2,000 a month minimum, five-year term. Vancouver hotels already sell C$40 sessions.

Purchase is the main path, and the 50 percent deposit pays for materials before each build. Sales tax matters in Canada: hotels and short-stay operators recover GST and HST, but condos and long-term rental landlords generally cannot, so we quote them with tax shown, and we will have a written BC PST ruling before we quote in BC. Flex removes the capital objection without putting leases on our balance sheet. The hotel revenue share is capped at three lighthouse hotels: it buys flagship case studies. Every path ends with the same two subscriptions. Seven-year gross profit is about C$76,000 on the hardware plus about C$44,000 on Care and Access.
