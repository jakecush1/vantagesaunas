Business model

Hardware sale up front, recurring revenue for the life of the building.

1. Purchase

50% at contract, 40% at delivery, 10% at commissioning. Deposits fund the build, so working capital stays light.

2. Vantage Flex lease

From $1,650, $2,950 or $5,200 a month over 84 months, Care and Access included. A finance partner pays Vantage in full at install.

3. Hotel revenue share (5 pilots)

$0 upfront, 35% of session revenue, $1,500 a month minimum, five-year term. Hotels price sessions at $35–$60 a guest.
