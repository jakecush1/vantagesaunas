The problem

Owners have run out of amenities to add.

Amenities decide leases

Gyms, lounges and coworking floors are table stakes. Owners need something residents, guests and tenants talk about.

The big options are gone

A rooftop pool costs millions and takes years. A spa floor needs plumbing, mechanical and space the building does not have.

The roof sits empty

A mechanical zone and nothing else, or a deck used three months a year. The best view in the building, unused.

Today a rooftop sauna means an architect, a structural engineer, a sauna builder, an electrician, a crane company, several permits and about a year. Nobody sells it as a product.
