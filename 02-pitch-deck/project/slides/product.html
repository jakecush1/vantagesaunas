Same wall panel, base frame, heater platform and software across all three models; only the footprint and the rooms change. That is what lets us build in a workshop and price before we visit the site. In every hot room both bench tiers run along the back wall and face a full-length tinted glass front, so every seat has the view; the side walls are solid wood and carry the door and the heater. The Lodge is the volume model: its shower is inside the changing vestibule and the lockers sit outside by the entry. The Summit is the hotel and flagship model. Its cold plunge is regulated as a pool in both provinces, so it needs dosing, logging and a health permit. Site preparation is quoted separately: in BC seismic anchors and flashing run C$8,000 to 15,000, and a 600 V building needs a C$10,000 to 25,000 transformer kit.
