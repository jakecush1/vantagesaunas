The solution

A rooftop sauna suite as a product: one contract, one price, 12 to 20 weeks.

All-electric. 208 V commercial heaters with a Canadian mark (cETLus, cULus or CSA). No gas, no flue. A transformer kit for 600 V buildings.

Factory-built in BC. Carried up the freight elevator as a flat-pack, or craned as a module. One to six days on site.

Engineered for the roof. Steel base for 4.8 kPa occupied roofs. Seismic anchors, flashed by a roofer, in BC; a ballasted base in Toronto where the site allows.

Run by software. Residents and guests book in the Vantage Access app; the smart lock and session timer do the rest.

Serviced for life. Vantage Care: quarterly inspections, heater stones, bench refinishing, 24-hour support.

We productized the custom project. The owner signs one contract at a from-price. We handle engineering, permits, the build in our Fraser Valley workshop, delivery and commissioning. In Canada the timeline is 12 to 20 weeks for a Perch or Lodge and 16 to 26 for a Summit, because permits, strata or condo votes and the pool permit for a plunge take longer than the build itself. In Vancouver and Victoria the base is bolted to the structure with engineered seismic anchors that a roofer flashes; the zero-penetration ballasted base applies only in Toronto, where the site allows it. In Ontario every heater has a hard-wired one-hour cut-off with no override, so the app books sessions inside that limit.
