# Launch plan: what to raise, what it costs, and the steps to launch

*Vantage Sauna Co. · Canada (Vancouver, Victoria, Toronto) · 1 November 2026 · all figures in Canadian dollars, before sales tax*

> **The short answer.** Raise about **C$2.15M of equity** in two steps: **C$150,000 of pre-seed now** and a **C$2.0M seed round by the end of March 2027**. Add a **C$350,000 government-backed equipment loan** and about **C$150,000 of NRC IRAP support**. That pays for engineering and Canadian certification, a demo Lodge, a Fraser Valley workshop, a team of four growing to six, and the first 19 installs through 2028. In the base plan the company is profitable on an EBITDA basis from 2029, and cash never drops below **C$0.84M**. The cushion covers slow permits, slow condo votes and a weaker year.

These are planning estimates from the quarterly cash model behind the five-year plan (see the financial model and the brief). Sources for wages, leases, crane rates, taxes and funding programs are in the Canadian research files.

## 1. How much to raise

### 1.1 Why C$2.0M of seed

The base plan installs 5 suites in 2027, 14 in 2028, 24 in 2029, 34 in 2030 and 45 in 2031 (122 buildings, about 11% of the suitable buildings in the three cities). Customers pay a 50% deposit when they sign, which pays for materials, so the money you raise mainly funds the team, engineering, certification and the workshop until installs cover them.

**Downside check.** The research's conservative case (81 buildings by 2031) reaches only break-even by 2031. With a C$2.0M seed the money still lasts through 2029; after that you would delay the 2029–2030 hires. That is why the recommendation is not the minimum.

## 3. Step-by-step plan

### 3.1 Your first 30 days

1. **Decide the name.** Run a trademark and corporate-name search for "Vantage Sauna"; pick a backup.
2. **Incorporate** a Canadian-controlled private corporation in BC. Open a business bank account. Register for GST.
3. **Hire two advisers** on fixed fees: a startup lawyer and an accountant who files SR&ED claims.
4. **Book 30 discovery meetings**: 10 hotel general managers and owners in Vancouver and Victoria, 10 rental-tower owners and asset managers, 5 strata management firms, 5 developers of new rental and hotel projects. Ask what they pay for amenities today, who signs, and what would stop them.
5. **Engage a BC structural engineer** for the seismic anchorage concept, and an electrical engineer for the 208 V and 600 V design.
6. **Call NRC IRAP** to meet an Industrial Technology Advisor, and check Futurpreneur eligibility.
7. **Request a written BC PST ruling** on whether a suite is equipment or an improvement to the building.
8. **Make the repository private** or confirm that public is what you want, before sending investors anything confidential.

### 3.3 Decision gates

- **End of January 2027:** fewer than three letters of intent means you change the offer before raising (for example lead with hotel revenue-share, or with the Perch only).
- **End of June 2027:** if the demo Lodge costs more than C$130,000 to build, redesign the panels before taking more orders.
- **End of 2027:** if fewer than three of the five pilots would buy again or refer, pause Toronto and fix the product and service first.
- **Mid-2028:** if gross margin is below 30%, raise prices or cut cost before hiring for Toronto.

### 3.5 Risks to watch in the first year

- **Permits and votes take longer than planned.** Quote 12–20 weeks and keep the reserve; sell to rental owners and hotels first because one person decides.
- **Certification surprises.** Use heaters that already carry a Canadian mark, and get the electrical inspector's view on the demo before the first install.
- **Seismic anchoring raises site cost.** Price it as site preparation and get a roofer partner who can flash anchors under the existing roof warranty.
- **Night-time privacy with tinted glass.** Ship a dim night lighting scene and offer an interior blind.
- **Public repository.** Anything pushed to a public GitHub repository is visible to everyone, including the financial model.
